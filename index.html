Design the wall art for your own home, to scale, with your session photos.

  1. Add your favorite photos — the panel on the left suggests good ones to include.
  2. Pick a wall — or snap a straight-on photo of your actual wall with a credit card or dollar bill taped to it, and load it with Use photo…
  3. Start from a template — then drag, swap and resize until it feels right.
  4. Send to Amy — your design and photos go to her by email, and she takes it from there.

Your photos stay on your device until you choose to send your design.
